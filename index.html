GPU Acceleration in Optimization and Optimal Control

Pre-congress workshop at the 23rd IFAC World Congress — BEXCO, Busan, Sunday 23 August 2026, 08:30–12:00 KST.

  • Lecture slides (PDF)
  • Hands-on notebooks

Discretizing an optimal control problem produces a nonlinear program that is large, sparse, and repetitive in structure, and recent advances in interior-point methods and sparse linear algebra make such problems solvable on GPUs. The lecture covers this in three parts: nonlinear optimization, optimal control as a structured nonlinear program, and GPU acceleration. In the hands-on session participants work through two notebooks — GPU computing in Julia, then optimal control and parameter estimation on GPUs — built on Julia, CUDA.jl, ExaModels.jl, and MadNLP.jl.

Basic background in optimization and control and some experience with a high-level scientific computing language are enough; no GPU programming experience is required. The exercises run on a hosted GPU environment (access given in the room), so bring a laptop with a web browser.
