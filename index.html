About

Discretizing an optimal control problem produces a nonlinear program that is large, sparse, and repetitive in structure, and recent advances in interior-point methods and sparse linear algebra make such problems solvable on GPUs. The workshop covers the theory behind these advances and the open-source software that implements them, and in the hands-on session participants formulate and solve optimal control problems on GPUs themselves.

About two hours of lectures are followed by about 1.5 hours of hands-on tutorials with interactive notebooks. Participants need basic background in optimization and control and some experience with a high-level scientific computing language; no GPU programming experience is required. A laptop with a web browser is enough, since the exercises run on a hosted GPU environment.

Software and papers

The workshop is built on Julia, CUDA.jl, and KernelAbstractions.jl, and the hands-on session uses two packages from MadSuite: ExaModels.jl for algebraic modeling and automatic differentiation on GPUs, and MadNLP.jl, a GPU-accelerated interior-point solver. A similar tutorial, focused on power systems, was offered at PowerTech 2025.
